Annual Supreme Court Citation Summary

October terms; selected argued majority and per-curiam opinions. New 2020–2023 datasets are preliminary: they have automated boundary and structural checks and targeted evidence checks, not complete manual citation audits.

Overlap is the number of distinct precedents cited in both terms divided by the number cited in either term (Jaccard similarity). Each precedent counts once in each set. This does not measure agreement, influence, or how frequently a shared precedent was cited. Gaps between collected terms are not treated as annual changes.

Mixed majority/plurality opinions and unresolved source records are held out. Unresolved citation references are logged and omitted. Flagged entries may repeat a precedent and may concern procedural orders; their number is not a count of missing edges or an estimate of recall. Review-entry counting also differs between the original and expanded datasets.

All four new terms use the same extraction implementation. The 2004 archive method and the earlier 2024 PDF implementation differ. Footnotes spanning section boundaries and unnamed short forms may be missed. A zero-link record means no links were extracted under these rules, not necessarily that an opinion cites no Supreme Court cases.

Descriptive selected subsets. Coverage, unresolved references, issue composition, and extraction methods can affect comparisons. Additional terms do not by themselves establish a historical trend.
