Data and methodology

The datasets contain selected argued majority and per-curiam opinions, including dismissals with no citation links. The case selector lists source opinions; outgoing citations were not collected for cited-only cases.

The 2004 data use Harvard archive links. The 2020–2023 data use a common official-PDF extraction process; the 2024 data use an earlier PDF extraction implementation. Unresolved and ambiguous references are omitted. The new terms have automated boundary checks and targeted evidence checks; complete citation recall has not been measured.

Selected subsets, not a complete historical series. Extraction methods differ between terms. A citation does not imply agreement or legal importance.

Terms refer to October terms, not calendar years. Citation ages use decision years; some historical dates have only year or month precision.

Dataset manifests, exclusions, review records, and acquisition scripts are available in the repository.
